# EverythingEbooks

## Write your book. Read it. Make it yours.

[Start writing at everythingebooks.org](https://everythingebooks.org/)

EverythingEbooks is a free, open-source writing workspace that runs in your browser. Write a new book, return to an unfinished manuscript, or bring in an existing ebook to work on again.

The focus is your words and an ebook that is comfortable to read.

**No account. No subscription. No tracking. No cloud storage.**

## Your book is an EPUB from the start

Your ebook is your manuscript. Work on chapters and sections in **Authors**, where edits save automatically as you type, and use **Save EPUB to Library** whenever you want to read and review your latest draft.

When you are ready, export an EPUB for ebook stores or a PDF for a paperback. You decide where and when to publish it.

## Start with Authors

EverythingEbooks opens in **Authors**. Use **File** to choose how to begin:

| File option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New EPUB** | Starts a new ebook. |
| **Import EPUB from your device** | Opens an `.epub` file from your device. |
| **Edit EPUB from Library** | Opens a book already in your EE Library for editing. |
| **Import DOCX as EPUB** | Brings in a Word document. |
| **Import PDF as EPUB** | Brings in text from a PDF. |
| **Import manuscript.txt as EPUB** | Brings in a plain-text manuscript. |

Books enter through Authors. **Save EPUB to Library** saves a fresh copy in Library and opens the Booksellers EPUB for reading.

## Write, organize, and save

Select the **Title Page** or a section in the sidebar to edit it. Add and arrange chapters, front matter, and back matter as your book takes shape.

- The **full editor** provides formatting and images.
- Reopen Authors to return to the top of the last chapter or section you selected in your working copy.
- **Writing mode** gives you distraction-free writing for the current chapter or section. Press **Escape** to return. Sections with attached audio use the full editor.
- Edits save automatically in this browser as you type, in both the full editor and Writing mode. Title Page fields and chapter/section headings also save while the cursor is still in the field—no Return, Tab, or separate Save button needed.
- Click the chapter title above the editor to change it. EE includes that heading in both EPUB editions, so you do not need to repeat it inside the chapter text.
- **Scenecraft** adds presentation and audio for the enhanced reader and web-ready export. Listen using the orange icon in Library or the exported web-ready book.

If browser storage cannot save an edit, Authors keeps it pending and shows a message with **Retry saving**. Keep the tab open until the problem is resolved. Automatic saving is local to this browser; keep exporting ZIP backups of your writing.

Use the Authors menu for **Library**, **Cover**, **TXT**, **DOCX**, and **X-Ray**. TXT and DOCX export your writing in those formats; X-Ray lets you look inside the EPUB.

## Keep your author notes with your book

**No Matter** is a place for reminders, research, discarded passages, alternate endings, or anything still taking shape.

These sections stay in the **Authors EPUB** and enhanced reader. They are excluded from the **Booksellers EPUB** and paperback PDF interiors.

Keep them while you work. Move material into a regular section whenever you want it to become part of the published book.

## Save and read in your Library

Click **Save EPUB to Library** in Authors to save your latest changes and put a fresh copy in your own EE Library. A brief confirmation appears, then the Booksellers EPUB opens at the chapter or section you were editing, even if that section is empty.

This saves your book in your own browser. It does not send it to Amazon, Kobo, or another bookseller. To publish for sale, export your finished EPUB or PDF from Library and submit it wherever you choose.

**Save EPUB to Library** keeps Authors open and reuses an existing Library tab or opens one for reading. No Matter author notes are excluded from the Booksellers EPUB; if you were editing those notes, the book opens at its title page instead.

**Menu → Library** leaves Authors without making a new Library copy. It closes the Authors tab when Library is already open, or takes the current tab to Library otherwise. Authors finishes pending automatic saves before leaving.

| Library control | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Book cover** | The Booksellers EPUB for reading. |
| **Blue book icon** | The Authors EPUB, including author notes. |
| **Orange icon** | The enhanced reader, with Scenecraft presentation and audio where included. |
| **Plus** | Ebook Plus, with exports and other book tools. Its Help button explains each icon. |

Return to Authors to make changes, then click **Save EPUB to Library** again to review the updated book. If you switch to an older Library tab yourself, use its **reload arrow** to see the latest saved books.

Library's search finds books by their details. The three-dot view menu changes the shelf layout and sorting. **Menu → Settings** opens Library preferences.

The **sun/moon button** changes the shared light/dark setting for Authors and Library. An already-open tab may need a reload to show the change.

## Export the version you need

Open **Plus** beside a book in Library.

| Export | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Booksellers EPUB** — green export arrow | The edition for ebook stores, without No Matter author notes or EE's author-only settings. |
| **Authors EPUB** — blue book icon | Your editable EE copy, including author notes and saved paperback cover settings. |
| **Web-ready ZIP** | An enhanced-reading version with Scenecraft presentation and included audio. |
| **6 × 9 inch PDF** | A paperback interior, with options for a table of contents and chapter headings. |
| **6 × 9 paperback cover PDF** | A separate front, back, and spine cover file. |
| **8.5 × 8.5 inch PDF** | A square paperback interior. |

For a 6 × 9 paperback, create the interior first so you have its total page count for the cover. **Include Chapter Headings** adds the titles stored separately in Authors; they do not have to be repeated inside your chapter text.

Paperback cover settings stay with the Authors EPUB. The generated PDFs are separate exports.

## Check your Booksellers EPUB

In Ebook Plus, **Check EPUB** checks the Booksellers edition and shows errors and warnings. You can export a readable text report.

The checker does not repair your book. Results are cleared when you close it. It works offline after the initial app setup has finished.

Ebook Plus also has **X-ray** for looking inside the Authors EPUB: its chapters, images, and other files.

## Your writing stays in your browser

Your books and working manuscript are stored in this browser on this device. They are not automatically shared with another browser or device.

Export an **Authors EPUB** to keep or move an editable copy of one book. Use a **ZIP backup** to keep your saved Authors workspace, Library, and settings together.

### Make a ZIP backup

Click the **stoplight** in either Authors or Library. You can also use **Library Menu → Storage: Export & Import**.

Choose **Export All Data** to export a ZIP backup. Keep these dated files somewhere you can find them.

Keep the tab open while exporting. Progress shows the files and data written, and you can cancel. Browsers with a file-save picker ask where to save first; Safari and other browsers use their usual file export. The ZIP can be imported in another supported browser. EPUBs, images, and audio are stored without being compressed again, so backups may be larger but take less work to create.

| Stoplight | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Green** | No changes waiting for a backup. |
| **Yellow** | Changes need a new ZIP backup. |
| **Red** | Changes still need a backup after two intervening calendar days. The last export date appears beside the light. |

For example, changes after a Monday export stay yellow through Wednesday and turn red on Thursday. If you have never exported a ZIP, the reminder counts from your first change. Reading alone does not turn it yellow.

### Restore or revisit a backup

**Import Data** asks you to confirm replacement immediately after you select a ZIP. It then shows two steps with file counts and sizes: checking the backup, then restoring it. Library & Authors are reset only after the backup passes verification. When import finishes, choose OK to refresh the page and see your restored data. Export your current work first if you want to keep it.

A successful restore starts with a green stoplight. New changes need a new backup.

To explore an older draft without disturbing your current work, open EverythingEbooks in another browser and import the backup there. Your dated backups become a history of your writing that you can revisit.

## Write online or offline

On your first visit while online, EverythingEbooks makes one attempt to prepare the app in this browser. The progress popup shows the files saved and verified. Its completion message stays visible until you choose **OK**. If preparation is interrupted, you can continue writing online; EE does not restart preparation on later visits.

The initial setup attempt is remembered in this browser. A different browser or cleared app storage needs its own initial setup.

Bookmark [everythingebooks.org](https://everythingebooks.org/) to return in this browser.

Use that bookmark in the same browser on the same device, online or offline. Writing, reading, and creating your exports continue to work.

**Nothing installs.** Your writing remains in your browser and available to export as an EPUB or ZIP backup.

**Menu → Update App - xxxx** in Authors or Library checks the published version only when you choose it. If you already have the newest version, **You’re up-to-date!** stays visible until you choose **OK**, without processing the application files again. If a different version is available, EE updates the app and prepares its offline files as usual. Choose **OK** on completion to reopen the updated app. A connection problem simply reports that the check could not finish; you can keep using EE and try later.

Once EE is set up in this browser, opening a bookmark or reconnecting does not run Update App or repeat preparation. Updates are your choice; you may keep using your installed release. The number beside Update App identifies the version currently open. Other EE tabs may need a reload after an update you choose.
